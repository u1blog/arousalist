Hair Pulling

What you need

Just hands.

What to do

With a partner

Gather a good handful of hair close to the scalp — pulling from the ends is uncomfortable and damages hair; pulling from the root is the sensation. Apply slow, steady upward or backward tension — not a jerk. Vary the pressure from very light (just feeling the roots engage) to more assertive. Try combining with restraint — head pulled back, neck exposed.

Things to explore

  • Does scalp tension feel pleasant, unpleasant, or interesting?
  • Is the vulnerability of having your head's position controlled part of the appeal?
  • Does slow, sustained tension feel different to a quick pull?
  • How does the giver feel about directing someone's position this way?

Why people love this

Hair pulling is positional as much as sensory — whoever holds the hair guides where the head goes. That directional, controlling quality is what many people respond to, often more than the scalp sensation itself. It's a compact act of dominance and submission that requires no equipment and no planning. The neck, once exposed, also becomes suddenly accessible — which is rarely accidental.
